Super+Shift+1…9          Move window there without following it

Differences from dwm
Hyprland workspaces are not dwm tags. These bindings remain unassigned:
Super+Ctrl+1…9 (toggleview), Super+Ctrl+Shift+1…9 (toggletag),
Super+0 (view all), Super+Shift+0 (tag all).
Master requires at least one master window. Its master count changes
among existing windows rather than retaining dwm's nmaster value.
Floating temporarily floats tiled windows. Returning to tile/monocle
restores them while preserving windows that were already floating.
New windows and windows moved here also become floating.
Layout selections and history reset when the configuration reloads.
Fuzzel and Alacritty replace dmenu and st. Waybar keeps its own mouse
bindings rather than reproducing dwm's ClkTagBar/ClkLtSymbol actions.

Bar: click sound for mixer; right-click to mute; scroll for volume.
Click NET for nmtui. Lock after 10 idle minutes; displays off after 15.
